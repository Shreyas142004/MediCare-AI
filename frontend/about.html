About MediCare AI

Making Medicine Information Accessible, Transparent, and Safe

Combining Google Gemini Multimodal Vision with a verified 149,000+ medicine database to help patients and caregivers read, understand, and safely manage medications.

Our Mission

Overcoming Packaging Illegibility

Every day, millions of people struggle to read tiny text printed on foil blister packs, confusing active chemical salts with brand names, or misreading critical expiration dates.

MediCare AI was engineered from the ground up to solve this exact problem: accepting clear photos of the back side of medicine strips, extracting printed text regardless of orientation, and providing verified, plain-language patient explanations.

Core Architecture

  • Multimodal Vision AI: Google Gemini API structured extraction
  • Local Fallback Engine: Orientation-aware CRAFT + EasyOCR
  • Dataset Scale: 149,267 deduplicated pharmaceutical records
  • Database Engine: MongoDB full-text search with SQLite FTS5 fallback
  • Pure Frontend: High-performance HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JS
Safety & Integrity

Our Anti-Hallucination & Separation Standard

In healthcare applications, automated fabrication of medical details can have severe consequences. MediCare AI adheres to strict architectural safeguards:

1. Strict Packaging Isolation

Batch numbers, manufacturing dates (MFG), and expiration dates (EXP) are extracted exclusively from the uploaded photo. They are never inferred or populated from the dataset.

2. Explicit Uncertainty

When text on a strip is obscured by glare or wear, our pipeline marks the field as uncertain rather than guessing the salt or dosage.

3. No Prescriptive Advice

MediCare provides informational educational content only. It does not prescribe medicines or calculate therapeutic dosages.

⚠️ Comprehensive Medical Disclaimer

The MediCare AI platform and its recognition results are provided for informational, educational, and reference purposes only. The information generated by this service is not medical advice, clinical diagnosis, or treatment recommendations.

Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking medical evaluation because of information presented by this software. Always consult a licensed physician, clinical pharmacist, or registered healthcare provider regarding any prescription, over-the-counter medicine, or health symptom.

🔒 Privacy & Credential Security

Your security is paramount. All uploaded strip images are processed securely through the backend application. Sensitive API credentials (including Google Gemini API keys) are stored strictly on the server and are never exposed in browser storage, client JavaScript, or frontend network traffic.
