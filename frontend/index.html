How It Works

From Strip to Safety in 4 Simple Steps

📸

1. Upload or Snap

Take a clear photo of the back side of your tablet strip using your camera or phone.

🤖

2. AI Vision Analysis

Gemini extracts visible printed text: medicine name, strength, salts, manufacturer, batch, and expiry.

🔍

3. Clinical Matching

NLP matches the extracted brand or salt against our 149k medicine database with confidence scoring.

🛡️

4. Understand & Save

Read simple explanations, side effects, and precautions. Save scans to your personal history.
